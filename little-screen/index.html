A few little details.

Can I add a longer animation?

The stock screen loops through 36 pictures at 10 frames per second. The studio fits animations into that 3.6-second loop. Loading a new pack replaces the previous animation.

Will this work with every USB screen?

This uploader is for MSU2 Mini compatible 160 × 80 screens. It checks the USB device and reported resolution before writing. Other resolutions and unrelated USB screens are not supported.

Does the recipient need the website?

Only to change the animation. A loaded screen plays independently from USB power. The website never sends your imported pictures or screen backups to a server.

Can I use my phone or another browser?

You can browse and download packs anywhere. Connecting a USB screen needs a desktop browser with Web Serial support, such as Chrome or Edge, and an HTTPS or localhost page.
